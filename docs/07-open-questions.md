## Two questions that are not really open

Worth recording so they do not get relitigated.

**Should the donor be a 964 instead of a G-body?** No. G-body supply is deeper (roughly 74,000 3.2 Carreras and 60,000 SCs built), the Australian index averages A$114,000 against A$175,875 for a 964, the 964 trend is up 37% while the G-body is down 19%, and the G-body's galvanised shell is the durability argument for a car meant to be driven hard in heat and dust. Even Wiedergeboren concedes the point on the record. Settled, see `decisions/ADR-0002`.

**Should the tub ever be replaced?** No, under any circumstances. Replacing it risks Individually Constructed Vehicle reclassification, a materially heavier compliance path, and it destroys the "restored, not built from scratch" story. Settled, see `docs/03-regulatory-and-tax.md` section 2.
