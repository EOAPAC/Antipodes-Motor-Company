The commissioning journey

Weeks 1–2

Commissioning Consultation

Define the character and use case — grand tourer or canyon carver — and settle the donor strategy: concierge-sourced or client-supplied.

Weeks 3–6

Design & Engineering Atelier

A tactile private-office session: leathers, paint, metal finishes, aligned with the engineering spec. It ends with the Commission Lock-in.

Months 2–4

Teardown & Foundation

Bare-steel disassembly, media blasting, structural reinforcement — documented in transparent technical reports.

Months 5–14

The Build Journey

Monthly milestone photography from the paint booth to the dyno cell, with an open-door invitation to the workshop.

Final 2 Months

Testing, Compliance & Delivery

Closed-track shakedown, final ADR certification, and handover in the presentation suite with a hardbound Build Book and bespoke luggage.
