# ADR-0001: Adopt the atelier as destination, the advisory business as the path

Date: 31 July 2026. Status: proposed, pending founder ratification.

## Context

Two commissioned business plans reach opposite headline conclusions. Plan A
recommends building cars as Antipode Motor Co. at A$975,000 ex-donor. Plan B
opens with "The recommended entry is NOT a car-building atelier" and recommends a
capital-light demand-generation, donor-brokerage and advisory business.

Read past the headlines and both gate the manufacturing commitment on the same
condition: proven demand backed by real money. Plan A says do not ramp "until
build one is delivered and certified and at least one paid foundation commission
is banked." Plan B says an equity position in a builder is worth considering only
given "a repeatable pipeline of ten-plus committed seven-figure buyers a year AND
an experienced build partner willing to take the manufacturing and ACL risk."

## Decision

Build toward the atelier, fund and de-risk the journey with Plan B's revenue
lines, and put hard gates between the phases.

- Phase 0 (weeks 1 to 12): clearance and foundations. No manufacturing spend.
- Phase 1 (weeks 6 to 26): donor brokerage and advisory earning revenue; launch PR
  against renders and the brand.
- Gate 1: commit to build one. Four criteria, in `docs/01-scope.md`.
- Phase 2: build car one as an instrumented prototype.
- Gate 2: commit to volume.

## Consequences

The advisory and donor-desk lines are not a hedge, they are the demand-sensing
instrument that Gate 1 depends on. Revenue arrives in the first two quarters
rather than in year two. Nothing in Phase 0 is hard to reverse.

The cost is focus: two business models run at once, and Jeremy carries both. If
the advisory business proves absorbing enough to starve the build programme, that
is a real failure mode and the gates are where it should be caught.

## Alternatives rejected

**Plan A as written**, straight to building. Rejected: it commits capital to
manufacturing before the three questions that decide the economics (R1, R2, R3)
are answered, and two of the three are answerable without a car.

**Plan B as written**, never building. Rejected: the repository, the brand work
and the founders' intent are all directed at an atelier, and Plan B's own trigger
contemplates building once demand is proven. Refusing the destination is a
different decision from sequencing the route to it.
