# ADR-0003: Settle the equity split and the services-agreement labour rate together

Date: 31 July 2026. Status: proposed. **Blocks Gate 1.**

## Context

Plan A prices build labour at 3,000 hours by A$120/hr, an internal blended cost
rate, producing a 34.9% gross margin. Plan A separately recommends an arm's-length
services agreement paying European Galleria "a market labour rate for build hours"
so Luigi is not "diluted twice (equity plus donated labour)."

These two recommendations are in direct conflict. Plan B records specialist
Porsche shop rates at A$160 to A$250 an hour. Substituting a market rate:

| Rate | Gross per car | Margin | Year 1 on 2 cars |
|---|---|---|---|
| A$120 internal cost | A$340,000 | 34.9% | +A$236,000 |
| A$160 market, low | A$220,000 | 22.6% | -A$4,000 |
| A$250 market, high | -A$50,000 | -5.1% | -A$544,000 |

Luigi's compensation can come through equity or through the hourly rate. It cannot
come through both without the venture funding it twice, and at the top of the
market range there is no gross profit to fund anything.

## Decision

The equity split and the hourly rate are a single negotiation, recorded in one
term sheet, with the margin consequences modelled before signature. Neither
number is agreed in isolation.

Structure otherwise as Plan A recommends: a new clean entity, Antipode Motor Co.
Pty Ltd, ring-fencing liability away from both existing businesses; a pre-agreed
deadlock tie-break so a 50/50 does not paralyse decisions; four-year founder
vesting with leaver provisions given the key-person dependence; and Baden Bower,
BruntWork and GRA services credited transparently to Jeremy's capital account.

## Consequences

If Luigi requires a full market shop rate, the venture must either restructure so
his return comes predominantly through ownership, or reprice above A$975,000 and
surrender the Wiedergeboren wedge. Both are survivable. Discovering it after the
first commission is signed is not.

Run `node model/model.mjs` with candidate rates before the term-sheet meeting, and
bring the table.
